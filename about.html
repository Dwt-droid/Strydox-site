Behind the Work

Reunited in a team specialized in technical consulting, we offer across electrical engineering and automation, and civil, industrial, agricultural construction, and structural engineering. We guide projects through integration solutions, planning and drawings, related studies and procedures, and monitoring and controlling work to ensure all aspects, including safety audits and risk assessments, are aligned with the project plan and quality standards.

Our team also covers the field of hydro power plant electricity production, providing on-site assistance for hydro generator auxiliary systems.

Strydox?

Strydox brings independent, specialized engineering vision to every project, built on years of direct work in industrial electrical systems. The goal is always the same, solutions that perform reliably in the field. From technical studies and drawings, energy distribution cabinet to automation panel wiring solutions, and to diagnostics and compliance reviews, we work as an extension of your team, bringing expertise exactly where it is needed. Strydox combines engineering experience with a bespoke approach, so every solution fits your operation.
