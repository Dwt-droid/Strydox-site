Fields of Action

Industrial

Industrial projects rarely involve just one discipline, they draw on energy planning, construction, engineering design, specialized systems, and compliance all at once. We support industrial and domestic clients across this full range, coordinating the technical planning and on-site oversight if needed.

Energy

Energy takes many forms, it cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed from one form to another. This principle underlies both nature and modern technology, and it is the foundation of how we approach energy systems, through responsible use of energy resources, through innovation and the promotion of efficiency, all crucial for ensuring a sustainable future and protecting the environment.

Construction

Technically speaking, construction involves teams of engineers specializing in topography, soil structure, and rock mechanics, alongside structural and design engineers, who determine how and where to build. Their work covers the civil, industrial, and agricultural building sectors, reinforced-concrete, steel, and masonry design, along with the studies and calculations that validate a project before ground is broken.

Engineering & Design

The five fundamental questions of an engineer must always be taken into account, expressed as, What needs to be done? (Solution/Project); How should it be done? (Method/Technology); Why is it done this way? (Technical Justification/Theory); What is it for? (Purpose/Utility); How much does it cost? (Budget/Efficiency). These five pillars guide us, guide the engineering process, ensuring that a solution is not only technically viable, but also economically justified and useful for the client or society.

Specialized Services

Our specialized consultancy is centered on auxiliary systems for hydroelectric generators propelled by Francis/Kaplan turbine , along with growing experience in solar installations, that call for specific know-how, and where we can offer support that general electrical services don't cover. Supporting renewable hydro energy integration, advice experimental analysis of circuits for DC/AC power conversion and inverter efficiency, switched-mode DC/AC power supplies, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and battery energy storage systems (BESS).

Electrical Consultancy

Electrical consultancy covers a wide range of professional services focused on the design, analysis, optimization, and compliance of electrical systems, by conducting studies regarding short-circuit, load flow, arc flash, power balance and electrical protections, creating electrical drawings for power distribution, lighting, and control systems for commercial, industrial, and residential projects. Consultants act as the "DaVinci" of electrical infrastructure, focusing on the theoretical and regulatory framework, supporting renewable energy integration, power distribution and automation engineering, and emergency power systems, giving expert advice on feasibility, cost estimates, energy efficiency, and project management from design through implementation.

Construction Consultancy

Good results start before the work begins. This is the direction we guide clients by checking the location and land conditions, access to utilities and physical constraints. Following the course of the project by calculating and choosing reinforced concrete or steel and masonry structures, and construction costs, including interior partitioning and coverings, submitting a 3D preliminary image of a building to the client attention. Then the construction execution project will be released, consisting of plans and drawings of the foundation, elevations, masonry, roof, and installation schemes.

Compliance & Safery

Meeting codes, standards, and safety requirements is not optional, it is foundational. We support clients with documentation, inspections, and ongoing guidance, performing risk assessments or audits, ensuring projects meet regulatory standards and norms compliant.
